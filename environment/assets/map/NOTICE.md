# Offline Bangalore Basemap Notices

The bundled `bangalore.pmtiles` archive is a geographic extract of the Protomaps basemap generated from OpenStreetMap data.

## OpenStreetMap data

Map data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and is available under the Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL). The rendered map must display `© OpenStreetMap contributors` and link to https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright.

## Protomaps

The archive was extracted from the Protomaps daily basemap build dated July 16, 2026. Protomaps basemap generation and PMTiles tooling are open source. Source documentation is available at https://docs.protomaps.com/.

## Fonts

The bundled Noto Sans glyph is distributed under the SIL Open Font License. The license text and source asset are maintained by the Protomaps basemaps-assets project.

## Sprites

The bundled Protomaps sprite sheets are derived from MIT-licensed Tangrams icons. Their source and license information are maintained by the Protomaps basemaps-assets project.
